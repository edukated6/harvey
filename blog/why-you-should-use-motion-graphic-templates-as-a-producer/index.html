Why You Should Use Motion Graphic Templates as a Producer

2026-08-15

One method that catches viewers eyes contains fast movement, bright, colorful elements and strategic typography. In television this element is mostly used to keep the average viewer on their toes during a newscast, football game etc.. In Lifestyle, graphics make up a third of the essential elements to an successful, engaging show. I just so happen to be a Producer who enjoys all aspects of Broadcast, so I indulge in my daily operations as a producer. When creating graphics in Adobe After Effects, Motion Graphic Templates or MOGRT is my go-to way to create show elements and here are a few reasons why you should consider MOGRTs for your workflow:

  • Editing Time efficiency
  • Consistent look throughout a show/podcast
  • Team members can share the same look on their projects

And it isn't difficult to get it started creating one for your show/podcast either. Here's a step-by-step tutorial while you are in After Effects.
Step 1. Open your After Effects.

Step 2. Find a project you've been working on or have existing graphics in.

Step 3. At the top, click Window and find Essential Graphics. Click this to activate the Essential Graphics Window in your workspace.

Step4. Click the "primary" dropdown to choose the Composition you want to make a graphic template out of.

Step 5. From here, you should grab elements, effects etc. from things on your timeline to drag and drop into your Essential Graphics Panel. For example, if you want the ability to change text on a graphic just grab the "Source Text" inside of your text layer to gain this ability when using in Premiere Pro.

Step 6. Name it, set a proper poster time and export the MOGRT to your local drive.

Be sure to check the "3D Renderer" option in composition settings to "Classic 3D" because After Effects doesn't allow "Advanced 3D rendering to export in MOGRT files.

Feel free to drop a comment if you have any questions or need help with MOGRTs.
