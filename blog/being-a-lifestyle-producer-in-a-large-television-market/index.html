Being a Lifestyle Producer in a Large Television Market

2026-08-15

Hi, I'm Ahmaad Harvey and I am the Producer of Jackson, Mississippi's Premier Lifestyle Show, Studio 3, Producer of Today at 11 and Executive Producer of Lawcall on Today at 11. Sounds like a lot of hats huh? I want you to know wearing a lot of hats is just the tip of the iceberg producing revenue driven broadcast television.

I've made 2 years professionally and about 6 years total in the creative arts and the things I've endured have made me solid in broadcast time management, show flow adaptability and communication through storytelling. A producer is more than a story gatherer and scriptwriter, producers have to actually see the vision of a show and insert themselves into the show psychologically. Yes, it is more of a mental game than anything.

I noticed when I was getting into producing there wasn't much information online about different ways to approach a live broadcast. This guy here isn't one to gatekeep, so I will be sharing what I know, so future producers seeing this or even potential customers of my services may understand what we truly do as producers. Please leave a comment if you have any thoughts about producing live broadcasts.
