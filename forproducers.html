All-in-one Media Production Solution.

forProducers is an all-in-one production software for all media professionals. At its core, it has an ENPS-style rundown system that seamlessly blends film, social & commercial elements that can assist you in creating organized, fast-turning media. Also included, production documents that fill itself from the rundown. From this automated production orders, budgets, invoices and more are created for you to send to clients.

      How a production moves through it

      Six stages, and each one hands its work to the next. Click through them. Every stage has a small working piece of the app.

        Will the script fit?

        Every segment has a script panel. It counts the words, applies the talent's pace and tells you if the copy fits the slot. Edit the script or move the pace to see.

        Questions

        Do I need an account?
        No. Install it and start a show. There's no sign-up, no login and no subscription.
        Does it need the internet?
        No. Projects live on your computer, so a bad studio connection can't take your rundown down with it.
        Do I have to use AI?
        No. You only use it if you set it up. If you do, it can run on your own machine through Ollama, or through an OpenAI-compatible service with your own key.
        Can I move a show to another computer?
        Yes. A whole project packs into one .fpshow file.
        When can I get it?
        There's no release date yet. I'd rather it be right than early. The launch will be announced on the blog.
