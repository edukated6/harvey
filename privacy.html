Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

This policy explains what information theharveyeffect.com (the “website”) and the forProducers desktop app and account service (“forProducers”) collect, why, who it is shared with, and how to have it deleted. Both are run by Ahmaad Harvey, an individual based in Mississippi, USA (“I”, “me”). Questions go to contactforproducers@pm.me.

The short version: I collect what is needed to run the website, your forProducers account and your subscription. I don’t sell your information, share it for advertising, or use advertising trackers. The rundowns, storyboards and documents you make in forProducers stay on your computer.

On the website

Visit statistics

To see which pages are useful, the website records visits with its own analytics, not a third-party analytics service. Your browser stores a random visitor ID that lasts until you clear your browser’s site data, and a session ID that lasts for one visit. With those IDs the website records:

  • the pages you view and their titles;
  • your device type (for example, phone or desktop);
  • the site or search that sent you here, and any campaign tags in the link (such as utm_source);
  • clicks on links and buttons, how far you scroll, and time spent on a page.

These records do not include your IP address or your name. The website doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

Forms

  • Contact form: your name, email address and message are emailed to me so I can reply. They aren’t stored on the website.
  • forProducers downloads: when you request a download, I keep your email address, your operating system, the plan you chose and your visitor ID, to send the download and to know who is using the app.
  • Blog comments: the name and comment you post, with your visitor ID, are stored and shown publicly once approved. The visitor ID is used to limit spam.

Fonts

The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when a page loads. Google’s use of it is covered by Google’s privacy policy.

Your forProducers account

Everyone who uses forProducers signs in, on every plan. For your account I store:

  • your email address and the name you give;
  • your plan, and if you are on a team, the team’s name and each member’s role;
  • your sign-in sessions, including the IP address and the browser or app details at sign-in, so sessions can be secured and ended;
  • one-time sign-in codes, which are sent to your email and expire shortly after.

Payments

Pro and Team subscriptions are paid through Stripe. You enter your card details on Stripe’s checkout page, and I never see or store your full card number. I keep the Stripe customer and subscription records needed to know your plan, its status and its renewal date. Stripe’s handling of your payment information is covered by Stripe’s privacy policy.

In the forProducers app

  • Your work stays on your computer. Rundowns, storyboards, documents, guest questions, people and locations are saved on your device and in files you choose to export. I don’t receive them.
  • Plan and update checks. About every 6 hours while it runs, the app contacts my server with your sign-in session to confirm your plan and to check for a new version. When an update is available, it is downloaded from my server.
  • AI drafting is optional. If you turn it on, the text of your request goes either to an AI model running on your own computer or straight to the cloud AI provider you choose, using your own API key. It does not pass through my server. That provider’s privacy policy applies to what you send it. Your API key is stored on your computer, encrypted where your operating system supports it.
  • Guest approval forms are files you send to guests yourself. Their replies come back to you, not to me.

How I use this information

  • to run your account, sign you in, apply your plan and bill your subscription;
  • to deliver downloads and updates and reply to messages;
  • to understand how the website is used and improve it and the app;
  • to protect the service from fraud, abuse and spam;
  • to meet legal and tax obligations.

I email you about your account, sign-in codes, billing and important changes to the service. I won’t add you to a marketing list without asking.

Who I share it with

I don’t sell personal information or share it for advertising. I use these service providers, who handle it only to provide their service:

  • Cloudflare runs the servers and databases behind the website’s forms and analytics, forProducers accounts, downloads and updates;
  • Stripe processes payments;
  • Resend sends email, such as sign-in codes and contact-form messages;
  • Google Fonts serves the website’s fonts;
  • the company that hosts the website’s pages, which keeps standard server logs.

I may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of users or the service.

How long I keep it

Account information is kept while your account exists. Billing records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require. Website visit records, download requests and comments are kept until I delete them or you ask me to. If you ask me to delete your account, I delete your account information and sessions, except billing records I am required to keep.

Your choices

  • See, correct or delete your information. Email contactforproducers@pm.me from the address on your account, and I will respond within 30 days.
  • Change your name or plan, or cancel, from your account page.
  • Reset the website’s visitor ID by clearing this site’s data in your browser.
  • Turn off AI drafting in forProducers under Settings → AI Drafting.

You won’t be treated differently for using these choices.

Security

Connections to the website, account service and update server are encrypted (HTTPS). The forProducers app stores your sign-in session encrypted where your operating system supports it, and checks that your license and updates haven’t been tampered with. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, I will tell you as the law requires.

Children

The website and forProducers are not meant for children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given me information, contact me and I will delete it.

Where information is processed

I am based in the United States, and my service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. By using the website or forProducers from outside the United States, you understand your information will be handled there.

Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I will update the date at the top. If the change is significant, I will also email forProducers account holders before it takes effect.
